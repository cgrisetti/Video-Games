# 13_PLAYTEST_KAIZEN_UPDATE_PROMPT.md — Playtest Feedback Overhaul (UI/UX, Visuals, Biomechanics, & Card Clarity)

## 1. Executive Summary & Design Mandate
This specification addresses real-world playtest feedback across 14 distinct areas, upgrading **Everwood (Oakshire Raids)** to eliminate developer HUD text, establish robust settings (`Invert Y-Axis`, `Toggle / Keep Sprinting`), overhaul the world map, fix skeletal clipping and anatomical constraints, replace "lollipop trees" with pastoral atelier foliage, refine running arm animations, separate Admin test tools from Player Inventory, clarify card mechanics, and introduce clear enemy level/danger indicators.

---

## 2. Granular Breakdown of the 14 Playtest Updates

1. **True Options Screen & Controls Menu:** Remove all top-of-screen debug text overlays and build a stylized, storybook-warm `OptionsMenu` featuring a **Controls** tab displaying clear keybindings and controller mappings for both Keyboard and PlayStation/Xbox controllers.
2. **Invert Y-Axis Setting:** Add a dedicated toggle inside the Options menu for camera Y-axis inversion, persisting via `SaveManager`.
3. **Toggle / Keep Sprinting:** Add a "Toggle Sprint" (or "Keep Sprinting") setting that maintains sprint momentum until a horizontal/backward movement input is detected or sprint is clicked again.
4. **Enhanced Map Interface:** Overhaul the map UI (`WorldMapPanel`) to include a clean map legend, named towns and quest locations, world map vs. zone map view modes, active zone indicator title, and a rotating player direction indicator arrow.
5. **Map Way-Markers Restored:** Fix the map raycast/click handler so selecting a location as an active navigation way-marker functions reliably.
6. **Skybox & Horizon Density:** Address sparse upper-screen vistas by procedurally generating volumetric storybook clouds, distant horizon ridgelines, and scattered background elevation meshes.
7. **Shield Clipping Fix:** Adjust bone attachment sockets and local transform offsets for `SHIELD` armor nodes to eliminate shoulder clipping.
8. **Weapon Clipping Fix:** Re-parent and adjust holster offsets for back-sheathed swords to prevent cape and coat clipping.
9. **Anatomical Biomechanical Limits:** Enforce strict joint limits (`Quaternion` slerp clamps and twist limits on shoulder and elbow bones) in `ActorVisuals` / skeleton animation trees to eliminate unnatural 180-degree backward arm rotations.
10. **Pastoral Ateliers & Foliage Overhaul:** Replace "lollipop tree" stand-ins with rich, layered Godot multi-mesh foliage (bushy oak clumps, weeping willow tiers, varied undergrowth ferns) fitting our pastoral storybook art direction.
11. **Natural Geographic Shorelines:** Replace egg-shaped placeholder ovals in `tools/build_levels.gd` with organic, hand-crafted or noise-perturbed natural coastlines, river inlets, and irregular boundary contours.
12. **Natural Running Arm Animation:** Update player run cycles so the right hand performs a natural athletic running swing instead of locking to the belt.
13. **Settings vs. Player Binder Separation & Card Clarity:** Decouple Game Admin Settings from the Player Gear/Card/Combo/Map menu. Overhaul card tooltips to clearly state what cards do, when to use them (Hearth-Rite vs. Sign-Flow), and their level requirements, with a distinct visual boundary between Player Inventory and Dev Admin tools.
14. **Enemy Danger & Level Badges:** Add clear floating level/danger badges (e.g., `[Lv. 3]`, `[Lv. 10 Elite]`) above enemy health bars to communicate encounter difficulty instantly.

## 3. Build Notes (Phase 12.5, built 2026-11-06)

All 14 items are in, as five increments (K1 to K5), each check-only clean. The smoke test passes all 718 checks; `tools/test_playtest_feedback.gd` holds the 33 new ones. Windowed screenshots were taken on a scratch save (the family save was never written).

### What each increment built

- **K1 (items 1 to 3):** the top banner shows only the join prompt and short notices; its old lines (the level's hint and keys, the network status) moved to the Developer Tools (K5). `OptionsMenu` (`src/ui/options_menu.gd`) opens on Esc or a tap of Options / Menu, over that player's view, with the tabs Audio, Video, Controls and Gameplay; the Controls tab draws the keyboard and the pad (PlayStation or Xbox names) from the Input Map. `SettingsManager` (`src/autoloads/settings_manager.gd`, autoload `Settings`) holds `@export var invert_y_axis: bool = false`, `@export var toggle_sprint_mode: bool = true` and the rest, saved as JSON through `SaveManager` (`src/autoloads/save_manager.gd`, written to a temp file then renamed). Toggle sprint: one click of sprint keeps running until the stick turns sideways or back, the hero stands still for 0.35 s, sprint is clicked again, or stamina runs dry.
- **K2 (items 4 and 5):** `WorldMapPanel` (renamed from `CartographyAtlasPanel`) shows a Zone Map of the island you're on, painted from that island's own builder, and a World Map of all five isles: a legend, named towns, dungeons, crossings and quests, the zone title in the header, the hero's turning arrow, and way-markers set by a click on a mark or open ground, or by the pad. A way-marker on another isle routes by way of its crossing. Map names are placed so they don't pile up (isles and towns first; a minor name with no room is left off until chosen).
- **K3 (items 6 to 9, 12):** the storybook sky (`src/actors/storybook_sky.gdshader`): painterly cumulus and cirrus at half resolution and three hazy layers of far ridgelines, its colours still eased by the atmosphere. `StorybookKinematicsModifier3D.limit_arms` clamps the shoulder's reach behind and across, the elbow's bend (never backward, at most 150 degrees) and the forearm's twist (85 degrees) on every pose; over the whole clip library no elbow bends backward (it did, by up to 100%). Both arms swing on the run. The greatshield is strapped across the forearm and stays clear of the shoulder, head and chest; back gear sits 0.31 m out, past the cloak, and straps the cloak's top down.
- **K4 (items 10 and 11):** Caldera trees near a camera (within 200 m of a landblock's middle) are sculpted atelier meshes instanced in MultiMeshes: an oak clump (a gnarled moss-oak with hanging moss and ferns at its foot), a slender ash, a weeping willow (new, on Oakshire's low shore and Barrow-Eyot's fen), an orchard apple in fruit, a pine of drooping needle tiers (snow on its boughs higher up) and a layered fern; past that, one-draw stand-ins. Every elliptical isle's shore wanders into bays, headlands and a ragged edge, and gets two to four river inlets (steep-sided firths on Rime-Firth), never near a quay, pad, ramp, road, arrival, named place or causeway anchor.
- **K5 (items 13 and 14):** the Developer Tools (`DevAdminPanel`, F1, or hold Share and tap D-Pad ↑) gather every test tool: the debug hero switch, ending a playtest, the F9 warp, host / join / leave and the old banner's lines, in a hazard-banded panel that is never part of the Binder or the Options menu. Card tooltips give three lines: what the card does; when to use it (on the trail, Trail-Prep: hold its button 2 s for INK READY; in a fight, Sign-Flow: tap it in the flash after a hit, with its pips); and whether your level and binder take it. Enemies wear `EnemyNameplate` badges (`[Lv. 3]`, `[Lv. 4 Rare]`, `[Lv. 10 Elite]`), coloured by danger to the party's level; the boss shows `[Lv. 5 Boss]` on its HUD bar. The Gameplay tab can hide the badges.

### Deliberate differences from the brief

- **Files outside the listed scope.** The brief limits changes to `src/ui/`, `src/actors/`, `src/autoload/`, `src/resources/`, `tools/build_levels.gd` and `tools/smoke_test.gd`. The repo's folder is `src/autoloads/`. These were also needed: `src/components/input_component.gd` (the Options and Developer Tools buttons, toggle sprint, invert Y), `src/levels/level_base.gd` (the quiet banner, installing the sky), `src/world/world_atmosphere_director.gd` (the sky's colours), `src/world/caldera/` (the trees and coasts: the lollipops and egg-shaped isles were there), `tools/build_input_map.gd` and `project.godot` (the new actions and the `Settings` autoload), and the `tools/test_*.gd` checks. Phase 11's landblock streaming (`src/world/landblock_*`, `world_grid_manager.gd`, doc 10) is untouched.
- **Coastlines in the island builders, not `tools/build_levels.gd`.** The live islands are built in code by `IslandBuilder` and its island builders; `build_levels.gd` makes the legacy maps. The coast reshaping and inlets live in `IslandBuilder` and apply to every island that gives a plain elliptical shape (Miller's Oak and the three friction isles). The Crown keeps its own hand-shaped coast.
- **Ridgelines in the sky shader.** The "scattered background elevation meshes" are three ridge layers drawn in the sky itself, so they cost nothing and never pop; the islands' own impostors still stand on the horizon.
- **Joint limits in the kinematics modifier.** The brief names `ActorVisuals` and animation trees; the hero has no AnimationTree, so the clamps run in `StorybookKinematicsModifier3D` after each pose, where every clip and the springs pass through.
- **Toggle sprint's stop.** "Until a horizontal or backward input" also ends on standing still for 0.35 s, so a hero who lets go of the stick doesn't set off running again.
- **Options on a tap.** Esc and Options / Menu open the menu on a tap, so holding Start still leaves the party as before.
- **Quest locations from the world's progress.** The Caldera has no quest givers yet, so the map's quest marks follow `CalderaWorldState`: the Root-Sanctum, then the Beacons, the trials and the Harmonic Bell.
- **The strap holds the cloak's top.** Back gear pins the cloak's upper rows down; the hem still swings.
- **The greatshield turned across the forearm** rather than slid along it; along the forearm it traded one clash for another.
- **Badges keep their size on screen.** Every other Label3D shrinks with distance (`WorldLabelStyle`, doc 18 §6.2); the enemy badge is the one exception, held to the screen out to 40 m so a family reads it at a glance. The test that guards the label style allows only `EnemyNameplate`.
- **Developer Tools key.** F1 on the keyboard; on a pad, the existing Share chord (D-Pad ↑) beside the debug switch's D-Pad → and ←.
