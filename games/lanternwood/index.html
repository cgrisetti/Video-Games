How to play

Follow the forest path. Behind the gates, archways and openings along it are games to play. Walk up to one and press F (□ on a controller) to see what's there, then press it again to go in.

Berry Rush is open now, and so is the Bramble Maze, through the old garden door at the end of the path. The others are coming soon!

Pick all 9 raspberries. Then your fox friend sniffs out the golden raspberry: follow it! The clock stops when you pick the golden one.

If an inch worm touches you, the round is over. Bonk one on the head with your stick and it's knocked out for a few seconds, but each bonk makes it a little faster.

You start by the old tree in the middle of the maze. Find the open garden door that leads out! There's a new maze every time, and the clock stops when you step out through the door.

Each corner of the maze is its own garden, with its own flowers. Lanterns light up at every crossing you've passed, and every dead end has something to remember it by. The old tree is in the middle, and the bell tower is to the north.
