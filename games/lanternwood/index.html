How to play

Follow the forest path. Behind the gates, archways and openings along it are games to play. Walk up to one and press F (□ on a controller) to see what's there, then press it again to go in.

Berry Rush and Gnome Crossing are open now. The others are coming soon!

Pick all 9 raspberries. Then your fox friend sniffs out the golden raspberry: follow it! The clock stops when you pick the golden one.

If an inch worm touches you, the round is over. Bonk one on the head with your stick and it's knocked out for a few seconds, but each bonk makes it a little faster.

Hop up the trail as far as you can, one hop at a time. Ride the logs and lily pads across the creeks (the water is too deep to wade), and dodge the deer, boars and hedgehogs on the animal trails.

Night is falling behind you. If it catches up, the owl flies you home to bed, so keep hopping! Pick raspberries on the way: every 8th brings out a golden one. Your fox friend points to it, and it saves you from one splash or bump.
